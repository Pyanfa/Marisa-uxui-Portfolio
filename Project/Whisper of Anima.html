Key Design Decision

Complex Game Play

Players seek more complex challenges without spending time learning entirely new systems.

Elevate difficulty by building upon existing mechanics, minimizing the learning curve.

Use initial objective cards combined with environmental lighting to guide players naturally without relying on text.

Adaptive Mechanics

Players experience a strong sense of satisfaction when applying their accumulated skills and techniques to solve problems independently.

Support flexible problem solving over rigid solutions.

Leverage element-environment interactions to support player-driven strategies.

Multiple Strategies

Players seek strategic experimentation and high replayability.

Enhance replayability by offering branching paths and meaningful player choices.

Design a multi-path level structure integrated with a randomized item system to deliver a unique experience in every playthrough.

Item Management

Playtest results show that players desire more interaction with enemies and find the current resources unappealing.

Enhance enemy encounter engagement while maintaining difficulty and overall game balance.

Add interactive items coupled with cross-level resource management, while strictly limiting item boxes in high-risk areas.
