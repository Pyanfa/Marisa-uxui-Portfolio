Project Title

Coral College

An interactive learning experience designed to educate marine tourists on personal safety and responsible tourism. It aims to make essential knowledge about marine safety and environmental conservation accessible, engaging, and easy to understand through a short and intuitive user experience.

Target Audience

Language

Users come from various countries and have diverse language backgrounds.

Children & Younger Users

May have limited attention for lengthy text.

Target

Primary goal is travel and leisure, with little time on a steep learning curve.

Tourism

Marine tourists of diverse genders, ages, and nationalities, specifically focusing on those engaging in coastal and offshore activities.

Background

International tourists carrying varying learning styles and mental models.

Older Adults

Older adults may have more visual limitations than younger users.

Technology Literacy

Varying levels of tech literacy.

Pain Point

Users lack clear guidance on eco-friendly and safe travel practices, as essential information is fragmented, difficult to access, and not optimized for quick understanding.

User Needs

Requires quick, frictionless knowledge delivery—presenting essential insights in an easily digestible and practical format.

Project Goal

Designing an intuitive learning experience that equips tourists with practical marine safety and eco-travel knowledge. The content is crafted to be effortlessly digestible and instantly applicable, seamlessly blending into their travel journey without feeling educational.

🚨

Challenge

Creating a universally accessible experience tailored for multi-cultural and multi-generational travelers. By leveraging intuitive interactions, strong visual communication, and a streamlined Information Architecture, the design minimizes friction, allowing users of all tech and language backgrounds to absorb and apply key knowledge instantly.

Usability Testing

Ao Chalong Pier, Phuket

Revealed key usability challenges

Long Learning Time

Users spent longer than intended with the app.

Combined 3 videos into 1 continuous video and reduced questions.

Low Button Discoverability

Some users had difficulty finding buttons near the screen edges.

Moved primary buttons to the center and increased their size.

Language Accessibility

Some visitors were less comfortable using English.

Expanded language options to better support a diverse range of visitors.
