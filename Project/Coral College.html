Project Title

Coral College

An interactive learning experience designed to educate marine tourists on personal safety and responsible tourism. It aims to make essential knowledge about marine safety and environmental conservation accessible, engaging, and easy to understand through a short and intuitive user experience.

Usability Testing

Ao Chalong Pier, Phuket

Revealed key usability challenges

Long Learning Time

Users spent longer than intended with the app.

Combined 3 videos into 1 continuous video and reduced questions.

Low Button Discoverability

Some users had difficulty finding buttons near the screen edges.

Moved primary buttons to the center and increased their size.

Language Accessibility

Some visitors were less comfortable using English.

Expanded language options to better support a diverse range of visitors.

Key Design Decision

Attention

The primary goal is vacation and relaxation, not an educational course.

Delivering essential information quickly without disrupting the travel flow or feeling like a lesson.

Delivering fast, digestible information through interactive content and subtle gamification rewarding users upon completion to boost engagement.

Diverse Users

Serving a multi-cultural and multi-generational user base with varying linguistic backgrounds.

Relying on a single text format or communication style creates significant accessibility barriers.

Leveraging visual communication, multi-language support, and simple interactions to minimize heavy text and break down usability barriers.

Accessibility Needs

Spanning a broad age demographic, presenting diverse visual capacities and varying degrees of digital familiarity.

Minimizes user cognitive load through intuitive, self-explanatory design—guiding users seamlessly without requiring guesswork or exploration.

Applies high contrast UI, clean and readable typography, explicit visual cues, and straightforward navigation to ensure effortless usability.

Learning In Action

Knowing what to do doesn't mean knowing how to do it; raw information lacks real-world application and context.

Connecting to real-world scenarios so users can seamlessly apply what they learn.

Scenario-Based Mini-Games: Interactive simulations that bridge the gap between learning and real-world execution through hands-on practice.
